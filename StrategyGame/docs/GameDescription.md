# Game Description

## Unit templates
1. Infantry: standard foot soldiers that are versatile in combat. Most effective against cavalry units.
1. Sappers: specialised units that damage fortifications in settlements.
1. Skirmishers: light infantry or cavalry for reconnaissance. Consists of melee and ranged personnel.
1. Dragoons: mounted infantry that move quickly across the board but fight on foot.
1. Cavalry: mounted units that move quickly and fight on horseback. Most effective against ranged units.
